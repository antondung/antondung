## 👨‍💻 About Me

Welcome to my digital space! I am a university student blending the logical world of code with the creative universe of marketing. 

- 🔭 **Currently Executing:** My role as a **Google Student Ambassador**, developing platforms like *toanthaybinh*, and conducting scientific research in Computer Vision (focusing on traffic image recognition via **YOLOv8 & EasyOCR**).
- 🌱 **Currently Learning:** Advanced Information Security concepts, algorithm optimization, and learning system design at **CMC University**.
- 🏆 **Milestones:** National Finalist in **Vietnam Young Lions** (Team TRÀ ĐÁ - Digital category) and **L'Oréal Brandstorm** (Team BINARY BLOOM).
- 👯 **Looking to Collaborate on:** Open-source AI/CV (YOLOv8, RAG) projects, scientific research, MarTech innovations, and design-integrated marketing.
- 💬 **Ask Me About:** Conquering marketing case competitions (The Next DMX, Brandstorm, Young Lions), building impactful AI/Drone projects, Python, C++, or my GSA journey!
- ⚡ **Fun Fact:** My brain operates in two distinct modes: strict logic for debugging C++ code, and absolute chaos when brainstorming creative marketing campaigns!
